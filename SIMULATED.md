# What is simulated

DealDesk is a fictional product (Project Harbor), shown as a prototype. The page says so in the hero, the invitation and the footer: "Prototype · fictional data · nothing is sent".

- **The product.** There is no backend and nothing is sent. Every name (Jordan, Pat Morgan, Alex Rivera, Sam Lee, Dana), firm, deal and message is fictional.
- **App screens.** Exported as PNGs from the Figma prototype section "v6 FINAL · C3 prototype" (G11, H5, G9, A1, G17, plus H7, G8, G13t, G15, G18, G19 saved in `public/assets/screens`). They are never rebuilt in code.
- **The numbers in the carousel.** 3 recipients compared, 2 things to fix, 1 tap to send, 4 message states come from the sample prototype only. They are not product metrics.
- **Avatars.** Initials only (PM, AR, SL, J, D). No photos, no stock portraits, no real people.
- **Video 1 (Jordan's story).** Placeholder: the poster is the Figma scene image and the video file is not in the project yet.
- **Not on the page, by rule.** No testimonials, logos, certifications, pricing or metrics. No claim of legal correctness, compliance or security. No "Approved" state: the states are Ready, Pending, Sent, Outdated.
- **Chips.** The hero chip that read "APPROVED" in Figma is rendered "CHECKED", to respect the no-Approved rule.

- **Videos.** The six screen recordings are of the Figma prototype running on a phone (fictional data, nothing is sent). They are sped up (1.2x to 2.4x) and trimmed for the web; audio is removed. The Motion reel is a promo animation made for the project; its audio track was removed for the web version.
- **Avatars (v2).** Illustrated, Memoji-style faces drawn as vector in code (src/lib/avatars.tsx) for Jordan, Pat, Alex, Dana and Sam. They are fictional characters, not photos of real people.

- **Jordan's film.** Contains an AI-generated character; the end card says so ("Prototype · fictional data · AI-generated character") and the page keeps that line. The sound version has music and sound design.

- **Deal team orbit.** The eight people (Pat Morgan, Alex Rivera, Dana, Priya Nair, Sam Lee, Maya Chen, Leo Ortiz, Adrian Soto) and their roles come from the prototype's fictional deal-team list. "Ask Dana to add" only changes the card on the page: no request exists and nothing is sent.
